# Battery Optimization & Background Execution Guide

To ensure background notifications and services run reliably without being terminated by OS power management, configure the following settings:

## 1. Enable Autostart & Background Permission
1. Open **Settings** > **Apps** > **Manage Apps**.
2. Locate and select **Alisha Bubbzieee**.
3. Enable **Autostart** / **Allow Background Activity**.
4. Confirm permissions when prompted.

## 2. Disable Aggressive Battery Saver Restrictions
1. In the App Info screen, scroll down to **Battery** / **Battery Saver**.
2. Select **No Restrictions** / **Unrestricted**.
3. This prevents background services and notification tasks from being stopped when the screen is locked.

## 3. Lock App in App Switcher (Optional)
1. Open the App Switcher / Recent Apps screen.
2. Tap or hold the app card and select the lock icon to keep the application active in memory.

## 4. Notification Priority & Alerts
1. In App Info > **Notifications**.
2. Ensure notifications, banner alerts, and lock screen visibility are turned on.
3. Set priority to **High** for timely delivery.
